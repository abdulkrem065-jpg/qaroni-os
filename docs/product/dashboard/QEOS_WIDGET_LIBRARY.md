# QEOS Widget Library

## Authorization: QEOS-AUTH-0086

### 1. Executive Summary
- **Purpose:** High-level narrative of business health.
- **Displayed Data:** AI-generated text summary (e.g., "Sales are performing above average, but cash flow is tight due to pending debts.").
- **Update Frequency:** Hourly / On-Demand.
- **Priority:** Highest.

### 2. Today's Sales & 3. Today's Profit
- **Purpose:** Immediate pulse of daily performance.
- **Displayed Data:** Total revenue, gross profit, comparison to yesterday/last week (trend indicators).
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time (WebSocket/Polling).
- **Actions:** Click to view Daily Sales Report.

### 4. Cash Position & 5. Outstanding Debts
- **Purpose:** Liquidity monitoring.
- **Displayed Data:** Cash in drawer, bank balances, total unpaid customer debts.
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time.
- **Actions:** Click to view Receivables Ledger.

### 6. Inventory Alerts
- **Purpose:** Operational continuity.
- **Displayed Data:** Items below minimum stock threshold, items expiring soon.
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time.
- **Actions:** Click to generate Purchase Order.

### 7. Best Selling / Slow Moving Products
- **Purpose:** Assortment optimization.
- **Displayed Data:** Top 5 items by volume/revenue, bottom 5 items holding up capital.
- **Update Frequency:** Daily.

### 8. Recent Activities
- **Purpose:** Audit and operational awareness.
- **Displayed Data:** Feed of recent transactions, logins, inventory adjustments.
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time.

### 9. Branch Overview & Store Health
- **Purpose:** Multi-tenant/location monitoring.
- **Displayed Data:** Comparison of sales and uptime across branches.
- **Update Frequency:** Real-time.
