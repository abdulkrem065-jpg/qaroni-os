## Role Profiles
- **Store Owner:** Financial KPIs, AI Summary, Cash Position, Profit.
- **Cashier:** Today's Sales, Recent Sales, POS Shortcuts.
- **Warehouse:** Inventory, Receiving, Low Stock, Purchase Orders.
- **Accountant:** Receivables, Payables, Cash Flow, Financial Health.
