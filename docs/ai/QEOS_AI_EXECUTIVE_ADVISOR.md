# QEOS AI Executive Advisor

## Authorization: QEOS-AUTH-0089

## Mission
The AI Executive Advisor is an Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform designed to assist decision-makers by understanding business operations, analyzing data, generating recommendations, explaining KPIs, and predicting risks.

## Capabilities
- Morning Executive Briefing
- Business Health Summary
- Financial, Sales, Inventory, Customer, Supplier, Branch, and Employee Analysis
- Operational Risks & Strategic Recommendations
- Predictive Suggestions & Natural Language Queries
