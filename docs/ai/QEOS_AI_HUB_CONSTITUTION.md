# QEOS AI Hub Constitution

## Authorization: QEOS-AUTH-0089 Rev.2

## Mission
To act as the Enterprise Intelligence Core of the entire platform. The AI Hub is a provider-agnostic, context-aware decision intelligence platform.

## Architecture
- **Unified Hub:** A single singleton service orchestrating Provider, Memory, Context, Permission, and Tools.
- **Provider Independence:** Vendor lock-in is strictly forbidden. Supports Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, Local LLM.
- **Explainability:** All insights and recommendations must include Reason, Supporting Data, Confidence, Business Impact, Suggested Action, and Risk Level.
