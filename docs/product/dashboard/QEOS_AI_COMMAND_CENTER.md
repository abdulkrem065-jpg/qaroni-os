# QEOS AI Command Center

## Authorization: QEOS-AUTH-0086

## 1. The Executive Advisor
The AI is positioned not as a passive chatbot, but as an active Executive Advisor. It analyzes the entire business state and provides proactive recommendations.

## 2. Interaction Modes
- **Proactive Briefing:** Upon login, the AI generates a personalized "Good Morning" briefing summarizing yesterday's performance and highlighting today's priorities.
- **Natural Language Querying:** "Show me the top 5 customers with overdue debts," or "What was our profit margin last month?"
- **Contextual Actions:** If viewing the Inventory module, the AI panel suggests reorder quantities based on historical sales velocity.

## 3. UI Integration
- **The AI Bar:** A persistent horizontal bar at the top of the workspace containing the daily briefing or critical alerts.
- **The Command Drawer:** A slide-out panel containing the conversational interface, chart generations, and deep analytics.
- **Smart Suggestions:** Buttons injected directly into widgets (e.g., "Draft Reminder SMS" inside the Pending Debts widget).

## 4. Analytical Capabilities
- **Trend Detection:** Identifying anomalies (e.g., "Sales of 'Product X' spiked 200% today").
- **Predictive Restocking:** Estimating when stock will deplete based on run-rate.
- **Cash Flow Warnings:** Alerting if upcoming payables exceed current cash position and expected receivables.
