# QEOS Merchant Product Blueprint

## Authorization: QEOS-AUTH-0085

## 1. Product Map
The QEOS Merchant Product encompasses the following core modules:
- **Dashboard:** Centralized KPIs, recent activity, alerts, AI insights.
- **Sales (POS & Orders):** Point of sale terminal, online order management, refunds, quotations.
- **Purchasing:** Purchase orders, supplier invoices, goods receipt.
- **Inventory:** Stock levels, transfers, adjustments, physical counts.
- **Products:** Product catalog, categories, pricing tiers, barcodes.
- **Customers:** Customer directory, credit limits, purchase history, loyalty.
- **Suppliers:** Vendor directory, ledgers, performance metrics.
- **Accounting:** Chart of accounts, journal entries, trial balance.
- **Financial Reports:** Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow.
- **AI Assistant:** Contextual insights, natural language queries, proactive alerts.
- **Settings:** General configuration, tax rates, currencies, payment methods.
- **Staff:** Employee directory, roles, shifts, access control.
- **Organization & Branches:** Multi-tenant setup, location management.
- **Audit & Security:** System logs, access history, security alerts.
- **Notifications:** In-app alerts, email/SMS triggers.

## 2. Product Principles
The QEOS Merchant Product adheres to the following core tenets:
- **Speed:** Sub-second interactions for critical paths (POS, inventory scan).
- **Clarity:** Uncluttered interfaces, strong visual hierarchy, progressive disclosure.
- **Consistency:** Uniform design patterns across all modules.
- **Minimal Clicks:** Optimized workflows for high-frequency tasks.
- **Enterprise Appearance:** Professional, trust-inspiring visual language.
- **Arabic First:** Native RTL support, culturally appropriate terminology.
- **Offline Friendly:** Resilience against network drops, background sync.
- **Touch Friendly:** Sized for tablet and mobile POS usage.
- **Keyboard Friendly:** Extensive shortcuts for desktop power users.
- **Accessible:** High contrast, screen-reader ready, WCAG compliant.

## 3. AI Experience
The AI Assistant acts as a co-pilot for the merchant.
- **Entry Points:** Floating action button (always accessible), inline contextual tips, dedicated insights dashboard.
- **Conversation Panel:** Drawer/Sidebar for deep dive analysis, chart generation, natural language querying (e.g., "Show me top selling items this week").
- **Suggested Actions:** Context-aware prompts (e.g., "Stock for Item X is low, create a purchase order?").
- **Business Insights:** Automated anomaly detection, trend spotting, financial health summaries.
- **Alerts & Recommendations:** Proactive notifications regarding cash flow risks, stale inventory, or unusual staff activity.
