# QEOS FISCAL YEAR CONSTITUTION

## PURPOSE
This document governs the definitions and rules regarding time-bound accounting boundaries within the QEOS platform.

## CHAPTER 3: FISCAL YEAR CONSTITUTION

### DEFINITIONS
1. **Fiscal Year**: The standard 12-month accounting cycle for an organization.
2. **Accounting Period**: A strictly bounded time frame (e.g., month, quarter) within a Fiscal Year for which financial statements are generated.
3. **Month**: A 1-month reporting boundary.
4. **Quarter**: A 3-month reporting boundary.
5. **Year**: The complete aggregation of all periods within the Fiscal Year.

### RULES FOR PERIOD STATES
1. **Opening Period**: The state of a period accepting active, validated transactions.
2. **Closing Period**: A transitional state where normal transaction entry is halted, but adjusting entries and period-end allocations are processed.
3. **Locked Period**: A state where no further transactions can be posted without explicit, highly-privileged un-locking operations.
4. **Archived Period**: A permanently closed state. The data is immutable and strictly read-only for reporting and audit purposes.
